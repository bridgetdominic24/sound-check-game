Recording Agreement

between OnyxWave Records ("the Label")
and Bootswidafur ("the Artist")

Article 1 — Term

i

This agreement shall remain in effect for a period of 52 weeks, commencing upon signature.

FairHow long you're locked in. 52 weeks is standard. Anything over 104 weeks deserves a better royalty rate in exchange.

Article 2 — Royalties

i

The Label shall retain forty percent (40%) of all streaming and sales revenue generated by the Artist's releases under this agreement.

High — negotiateThey keep 40¢ of every $1 you earn. Typical range is 15–35%. Counter with 25–30%, especially with your fan count.

Article 3 — Advance

i

The Label shall pay the Artist an advance of $12,000, recoupable against future royalties.

Read carefullyFree money now — but "recoupable" means the label repays itself from YOUR earnings before you see royalties. You earn $0 in royalties until $12,000 is paid back.

Article 4 — Release Approval

i

All releases require written approval of the Label prior to distribution.

Trade-offYou lose the freedom to drop music whenever you want. In exchange the label funds and promotes releases it approves.

Article 5 — Distribution

i

The Label shall provide full distribution to all major streaming platforms at no cost to the Artist.

PerkIndies pay yearly distributor fees for this. Signed = it's covered. This is one of the main reasons to sign.
